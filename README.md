A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry for **Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest** — one short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance, written to be judged by a furious judge with a famously short temper.

## The rant

> My microwave beeps when the food is done. Fair enough. Then it beeps again thirty seconds later. And again. And again, like it's worried I forgot I'm hungry. I did not forget. I am standing right here, fork in hand, staring at it, and it still beeps at me like I'm the unreliable one in this relationship. I paid for you. I feed you electricity. Trust me to hear the first beep.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt`. The rant is 378 characters of UTF-8 text — inside the 1–400 character gate.

Run it locally:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
